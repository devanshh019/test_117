ASME BOILER & PRESSURE VESSEL CODE (BPVC)
Section VIII Division 1: Rules for Construction of Pressure Vessels

Part UG: General Requirements for All Methods of Construction
UG-27: Thickness of Shells Under Internal Pressure

For cylindrical shells, the minimum required thickness (t_min) is determined by:
    t_min = (P * R) / (S * E - 0.6 * P)

Where:
- P: Internal design pressure (MPa or psi).
- R: Inside radius of the shell course under consideration (mm or inches).
- S: Maximum allowable stress value of material at design temperature (MPa or psi).
- E: Joint efficiency for longitudinal joints (typically 0.85 for spot RT, 1.0 for full RT).

Safety Factor & Joint Inspection:
All circumferential and longitudinal weld seams shall be inspected using non-destructive testing (NDT). If operating temperature exceeds 300 deg C, creep and thermal fatigue limits must be evaluated.
