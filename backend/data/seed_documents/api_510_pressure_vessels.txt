API STANDARD 510: PRESSURE VESSEL INSPECTION CODE
Maintenance Inspection, Rating, Repair, and Alteration

Section 7.1: Determination of Corrosion Rate and Remaining Life
The corrosion rate (Cr) shall be calculated using the following formula:
    Cr = (t_initial - t_actual) / Time_in_service

The remaining life (RL) of the vessel in years is calculated as:
    RL = (t_actual - t_minimum) / Cr

Where:
- t_initial: Thickness measured at initial baseline inspection (mm or inches).
- t_actual: Thickness measured at current ultrasonic testing (UT) inspection.
- t_minimum: Minimum required thickness calculated per ASME Section VIII Div 1.
- Cr: Corrosion rate (mm/year or inches/year).

Mandatory Directives:
If remaining life is less than 2.0 years, the inspection interval shall be shortened and an immediate maintenance turnaround schedule must be approved by the Asset Integrity Engineer.
